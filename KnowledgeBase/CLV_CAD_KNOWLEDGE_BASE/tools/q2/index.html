AERIAL

Aerial imagery controls for loading, replacing, and unloading available Nearmap aerial layers.

AERIAL

Controls for loading and unloading available Nearmap aerial imagery layers.

Tool
Purpose
UNLOAD AERIAL
This will unload all aerial layer groups displaying. Basically turning off all aerial images.
LOAD AERIAL
User can double click on the aerial date of choice. Once selected, that aerial will load. The user can double click another date, and the current image loaded will automatically unload, and the newly selected image will replace it. When a new aerial is selected, the view may zoom out to a new location. You may have to zoom previous to get back to the site location.
